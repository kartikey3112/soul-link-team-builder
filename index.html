    How scoring works

    Each Pokémon's base stat total is turned into a 0 to 100 power score, where 200 or less counts as 0 and 600 or more counts as 100. A player's raw stats are the average power of their six. If a player plans more than one Mega, only the single biggest Mega boost counts, because only one Pokémon can Mega Evolve per battle. Type balance is half coverage (how many types the team can hit super effectively with its own types) and half defence, which drops when several team members share a weakness that nobody on the team resists. Each player's team score mixes raw stats and type balance using the "What matters more" slider, and the overall team score is the average of the players' scores, shifted toward one player if you choose to favour them. The planner then searches every legal combination of usable links, always prefers a bigger legal team, and shows the five best.
